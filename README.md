Replacing a front door involves more than selecting a design that looks good. The door needs to fit the opening correctly, operate smoothly, provide dependable security, resist outdoor conditions, and complement the architecture of the property. Today’s Entry Doors brings these factors together by providing homeowners with guidance from the initial consultation through the completed installation.

The company offers a broad selection of fiberglass entry doors suitable for different architectural styles found throughout Orange County. Options include modern and contemporary front doors, Craftsman entry doors, traditional designs, rustic doors, Dutch doors, double Dutch doors, double entry doors, 42-inch doors, and oversized 8-foot entry doors. Homeowners can also explore doors with sidelights, decorative glass, and wrought iron-inspired details when they want to create a more distinctive entrance.

Fiberglass has become a practical option for homeowners who appreciate the appearance of wood but want a front door that requires less maintenance. Quality fiberglass entry doors are designed to resist many common issues associated with exterior doors, including warping, cracking, fading, and deterioration from prolonged exposure to the elements. The material can also provide good insulation when the complete entry system is properly selected and installed.

Today’s Entry Doors offers products from recognised manufacturers such as Therma-Tru, Masonite, JELD-WEN, and Plastpro. Working with multiple established manufacturers gives homeowners access to different door styles, finishes, glass designs, configurations, and sizes rather than limiting them to one narrow collection.

One of the company’s most useful services is its Mobile Showroom. Instead of requiring homeowners to visit multiple stores or choose an important exterior feature entirely from online photographs, Today’s Entry Doors can bring full-size door displays and product samples directly to the home.

This approach allows customers to compare options while looking at their actual entrance. They can consider how a modern door might work with the exterior, whether a Craftsman design better matches the property, how different finishes appear in natural daylight, or whether decorative glass and sidelights would improve the overall look of the entryway. Hardware and other available features can also be reviewed during the selection process.

The in-home consultation is especially valuable because every entrance is different. Door height and width, the existing frame, sidelights, surrounding walls, architectural details, exterior colours, and other factors may influence which replacement door works best. Taking these details into account before ordering can help create a finished entrance that looks intentional and functions correctly.

Professional front door installation is included as an important part of the company’s service. Today’s Entry Doors does not simply provide homeowners with a new door and leave them to arrange installation separately. Its team handles the installation process, including the fitting and alignment needed for the new entry system to perform properly.

Correct installation can affect several aspects of a front door, including security, weather resistance, insulation, smooth operation, and longevity. A door that is poorly aligned may become difficult to open or close, while gaps around an improperly fitted frame can affect weather sealing. Professional installation helps ensure that the complete entry system performs as intended.

Security is another area addressed during installation. Today’s Entry Doors uses its Safe & Secure installation process, which incorporates a 16-gauge steel security plate around the locking area and connects it to the home’s framing. Reinforcing this critical area provides an additional security-focused feature as part of the overall door replacement project.
